ABOUT

Engineering discipline, paired with a learner’s curiosity.

Hey there, I’m Zareen Khan, a passionate and results-driven Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) with over 10 years of experience as a DevOps engineer. Currently, I work at Cisco, where I’ve been part of the SRE team for more than 3 years, contributing to the reliability, scalability and performance of critical systems.

My professional journey includes roles at leading organizations like Comcast, McAfee, and CVS Health, where I developed a strong foundation in DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, cloud platforms, infrastructure automation and system monitoring.

With over a decade of expertise, I’ve honed my skills in building scalable, efficient, and innovative solutions. Based in the vibrant California Bay Area, I am deeply passionate about technology and its power to shape the future.

My commitment to sharing knowledge is at the core of my mission—empowering others to grow and succeed in their careers. I’m dedicated to driving operational excellence, fostering a culture of reliability and continuously learning.
